Editorial Policy

How we write and review our guides

The guides on this site are written and reviewed by the Avelogic Payroll Team: the people who set up, run and support HRKaki payroll for Singapore employers every month. Each guide is checked against the official CPF Board, IRAS, MOM, MOE or PDPC source, shows the date it was last reviewed, and is reviewed again whenever the rules change.

Who writes the guides

Our guides come from the Avelogic Payroll Team, part of Avelogic Pte Ltd (UEN 200604587R), a Singapore software company that has developed and supported payroll and HR software for local employers since 2006. The team works with CPF, IRAS, MOM and leave rules daily: configuring payroll for new customers, running monthly payroll for outsourcing clients, and answering customers' compliance questions.

Where the facts come from

  • Rates, ceilings, deadlines and legal requirements come from the agency that sets them: the CPF Board, IRAS, MOM, MOE, PDPC or another Singapore government source.
  • Each guide links to those sources, labels tables with their source, and names the agencies it relies on.
  • Where we explain how a rule works in practice, we use worked examples with stated assumptions so you can check the arithmetic.

How guides are reviewed

  • Every guide shows a Last reviewed date: the date its facts were last checked against the official sources.
  • Every guide shows a Next review date. Most CPF, IRAS and MOM changes take effect in January, so we review all guides each January.
  • When an agency announces a change between reviews, we update the affected guides and their review dates as soon as practical.

What our guides are, and aren't

Our guides are general information to help Singapore employers understand their obligations. They are not legal, tax or financial advice, and they don't replace the official source. For a decision about a specific employee or situation, check with the relevant agency or a qualified adviser. Avelogic is not affiliated with any of the agencies we cite.

Where it's relevant, a guide explains how HRKaki or our payroll outsourcing service handles the topic. Those sections are clearly about our own products.

Corrections

If you spot something that's wrong or out of date, email support@avelogic.com with the page and the issue. We check every report against the official source and correct the guide if needed.
