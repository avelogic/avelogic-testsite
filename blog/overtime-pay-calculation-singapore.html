Employment Act

How to calculate overtime pay in Singapore under MOM rules

Who is entitled to overtime pay in Singapore, the hourly rate formula, the $2,600 cap for non-workmen, the 72-hour monthly limit, rest day pay and the 14-day payment deadline, with worked examples.

By Avelogic Payroll & HR TeamUpdated 13 October 20266 min read

Key takeaways

  • Overtime pay applies to workmen earning up to $4,500 basic a month and other employees earning up to $2,600.
  • For monthly-rated staff, the hourly basic rate is (12 × monthly basic salary) ÷ (52 × 44). Overtime is paid at least 1.5 times that.
  • Overtime is capped at 72 hours a month, and total work at 12 hours a day.
  • Overtime must be paid within 14 days after the end of the salary period.

Who is entitled to overtime pay

The overtime rules in Part 4 of the Employment Act cover:

  • Workmen (mainly manual work) earning a basic monthly salary of $4,500 or less; and
  • other employees (non-workmen) earning a basic monthly salary of $2,600 or less.

"Basic salary" here excludes overtime, bonuses, AWS, productivity incentives, reimbursements and all allowances. Managers and executives aren't covered by Part 4, so whether they get overtime depends on their contract.

Normal working hours

Work patternNormal hours
5 days a week or fewerUp to 9 hours a day or 44 hours a week
More than 5 days a weekUp to 8 hours a day or 44 hours a week
Alternating weeksUp to 48 hours in a week, and no more than 88 hours over 2 weeks
Shift workUp to 12 hours a day, averaging 44 hours a week over 3 weeks

Source: Ministry of Manpower (MOM)

Any work beyond normal hours is overtime. Breaks don't count as working time.

The overtime formula

Overtime is paid at at least 1.5 times the hourly basic rate. Work out the hourly basic rate like this:

How the employee is paidHourly basic rate
Monthly-rated(12 × monthly basic salary) ÷ (52 × 44)
Daily-ratedDaily basic pay ÷ normal hours per day
Piece-ratedWeekly basic pay ÷ hours worked that week

Source: Ministry of Manpower (MOM)

Cap for non-workmen: for non-workmen, the overtime rate is capped at the $2,600 salary level. MOM states this as $13.60 an hour, even if the employee earns less than $2,600 but works on a different basis.

Worked examples

Example 1, MOM's own: a non-workman earning $2,600 works 2 hours of overtime. Overtime pay = $13.60 × 1.5 × 2 = $40.80.

Example 2: a workman earning $2,000 basic a month works 10 hours of overtime. Hourly basic rate = (12 × $2,000) ÷ (52 × 44) = $24,000 ÷ 2,288 = $10.49. Overtime pay = $10.49 × 1.5 × 10 = $157.35.

Limits on hours

  • No more than 12 hours of work a day, except in situations such as accidents, essential services or urgent machinery work. Employers can apply to MOM for an exemption of up to 14 hours.
  • No more than 72 hours of overtime a month, unless MOM grants an exemption.
  • Work on a rest day or public holiday doesn't count towards the 72 hours, except hours beyond normal daily hours on those days.

Pay for working on a rest day

Employees covered by Part 4 get one rest day a week. If they work on it, pay depends on who asked:

Who asked for the workUp to half normal daily hoursMore than halfBeyond normal daily hours
Employer1 day's salary2 days' salary2 days' salary + overtime
EmployeeHalf a day's salary1 day's salary1 day's salary + overtime

Source: Ministry of Manpower (MOM)

When to pay overtime

Overtime must be paid within 14 days after the last day of the salary period, and shown on the itemised payslip with the overtime hours and the overtime period.

Frequently asked questions

Who is entitled to overtime pay in Singapore?

Workmen earning a basic salary of up to $4,500 a month and other employees earning up to $2,600 a month, under Part 4 of the Employment Act. Managers and executives depend on their contract.

What is the overtime rate in Singapore?

At least 1.5 times the hourly basic rate. For monthly-rated employees, the hourly basic rate is (12 × monthly basic salary) ÷ (52 × 44).

What is the maximum overtime per month?

72 hours a month, unless MOM grants an exemption.

When must overtime be paid?

Within 14 days after the last day of the salary period.
