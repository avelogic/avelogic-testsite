Payroll & CPF

AWS, year-end bonuses and CPF on Additional Wages: an employer's guide

Whether AWS (the 13th-month bonus) is compulsory in Singapore, how pro-rating works, how CPF applies to bonuses through the Additional Wage ceiling, with worked examples, and how bonuses are taxed.

By Avelogic Payroll & HR TeamUpdated 13 October 20266 min read

Key takeaways

  • AWS isn't required by law. Whether staff get it, and how it's pro-rated, depends on the contract or handbook.
  • Bonuses are Additional Wages for CPF. CPF applies only up to the Additional Wage ceiling.
  • The AW ceiling is $102,000 minus the employee's Ordinary Wages subject to CPF for the year.
  • High earners can end up paying CPF on only part of their bonus.

Is AWS compulsory?

No. MOM's position is that the Annual Wage Supplement (AWS), often called the 13th-month bonus, is not required by law. An employee is entitled to it only if their employment contract, company handbook or collective agreement says so.

Pro-rating AWS

There's no statutory formula for pro-rating. Whether staff with less than 12 months' service, or who leave before the payout date, get a pro-rated AWS depends on the contract. Set the rule clearly in your KETs or handbook, for example "pro-rated for employees with at least 3 months' service who are employed on 31 December".

Ordinary Wages vs Additional Wages

Ordinary Wages (OW)Additional Wages (AW)
What it isWages for work done in the month, due by the 14th of the following monthAll other wages, such as annual bonuses, AWS and leave encashment
CPF ceiling$8,000 a month from 1 January 2026$102,000 − total OW subject to CPF in the year
When CPF is dueFor the month the wages are forFor the month the bonus is paid

Source: CPF Board

How bonuses are taxed

Bonuses are taxable employment income and are reported on the employee's IR8A. Timing depends on the type of bonus. A non-contractual bonus is taxed in the year it's paid: IRAS's example is a bonus announced in December 2022 and paid in February 2023, which is taxed for Year of Assessment 2024. A bonus the employee is contractually entitled to can be taxed in the year the entitlement arises, which may differ from the year it's paid.

National Wages Council guidelines

The National Wages Council (NWC) issues yearly guidelines on wage increases and bonuses. The NWC met in August 2026 and plans to release its 2026/2027 guidelines, covering 1 December 2026 to 30 November 2027, by the end of October 2026. Check the MOM website for the published recommendations before setting this year's bonuses.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 13th-month bonus (AWS) compulsory in Singapore?

No. AWS is not required by law. Employees are entitled to it only if their contract, handbook or collective agreement provides for it.

Do employers pay CPF on bonuses?

Yes. Bonuses are Additional Wages, and CPF is payable on them up to the Additional Wage ceiling of $102,000 minus the Ordinary Wages subject to CPF in the year.

What is the Additional Wage ceiling for 2026?

$102,000 minus the total Ordinary Wages subject to CPF for the year. With the Ordinary Wage ceiling at $8,000 a month, an employee earning $8,000 or more every month has an AW ceiling of $6,000.

Are bonuses taxable?

Yes. Bonuses are employment income and are reported on the IR8A. A non-contractual bonus is taxed in the year it's paid.
