What HRKaki payroll does each month

Running payroll in Singapore means getting a lot of small rules right at the same time: CPF rates that change with age, a wage ceiling that has risen every year since 2023, levies, self-help group funds, overtime limits and payslip rules. HRKaki handles these for you, so your monthly run becomes a review instead of a rebuild.

  • Gross-to-net calculation for monthly, daily and ad-hoc pay, with allowances, deductions, bonuses and MOM-rule overtime.
  • CPF, SDL and SHG funds (CDAC, SINDA, MBMF and ECF) calculated per employee, with rate changes applied by us when the CPF Board announces them.
  • GIRO bank files for DBS IDEAL, UOB IBG, OCBC Velocity, HSBC, Maybank, Citibank, SMBC, MUFG, BNP and Bank of America.
  • Itemised payslips that include every item MOM lists, sent to staff automatically and kept online for them to view.
  • Multiple pay cycles in one month, with CPF and statutory contributions recalculated correctly across them.

Year-end IRAS submission without the January scramble

Employers in the IRAS Auto-Inclusion Scheme (AIS) must send their employees' employment income by 1 March each year. HRKaki keeps twelve months of consistent records and submits the IR8A and Appendix 8A/8B data directly to IRAS. When a foreign employee leaves, it also prepares the IR21 for tax clearance. Read our IR8A and AIS guide for the full process.

HR records and leave in the same system

Because HR, leave and payroll share one set of employee records, nothing has to be re-keyed. HRKaki stores biodata, family and bank details, documents such as NRIC and passport copies, assets issued, education, offences and incidents. Leave policies support tiered entitlement by grade and length of service, with calendar views for planning and reminders for work pass expiry, confirmation and retirement dates.

FAQ

Payroll & HR Software: frequently asked questions

Is HRKaki payroll software CPF compliant?

Yes. HRKaki calculates CPF by age band and the Ordinary Wage and Additional Wage ceilings, plus SDL and self-help group funds. When the CPF Board changes rates or ceilings, we update the system for you at no extra charge.

Can HRKaki submit IR8A to IRAS directly?

Yes. HRKaki submits employment income records for the Auto-Inclusion Scheme directly to IRAS, covering the IR8A and Appendix 8A/8B, and prepares IR21 tax clearance for departing foreign employees.

Which banks does HRKaki create GIRO files for?

DBS IDEAL, UOB IBG, OCBC Velocity, HSBC, Maybank, Citibank, SMBC, MUFG, BNP and Bank of America.

How is HRKaki priced?

HRKaki is priced per employee per month, with no licence fee, no hardware and no long-term lock-in. Data migration is free. Contact us for a quote based on your headcount and modules.

How long does it take to switch to HRKaki?

Most companies run their first payroll within a few weeks. We migrate your employee records, leave balances and salary details for you, and your kaki reviews the first run with you before anything is finalised.
