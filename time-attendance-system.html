How HRKaki stops buddy punching

Buddy punching is when one employee clocks in for another. HRKaki blocks it in three ways. AWS facial recognition checks that the face at clock-in matches the employee. Each account is tied to that employee's registered phone, so a colleague can't use their own phone to clock someone else in. And the clock-in time is checked against both the phone and the server, so changing the phone's clock doesn't help.

From clock-in to payslip

Attendance records feed HRKaki payroll directly, including overtime under MOM rules and attendance incentive policies. With the optional e-Schedule module you can plan shifts and off days, and approved leave updates the roster automatically.

FAQ

Time Attendance System: frequently asked questions

Do staff need to install an app?

Yes. Staff use the HRKaki Attendance app on their own smartphone. Each account is locked to one registered phone. For staff without smartphones, use the Model 1010-LITE face recognition terminal.

How accurate is beacon-based attendance?

Scans only register when the phone is within about 5 metres of the beacon, so staff must be on site to clock in.

Does it work for construction and field teams?

Yes. GPS mode lets staff clock in at any location, and admins can see the location and selfie for every scan.

Does attendance link to payroll and overtime?

Yes. Attendance records flow into HRKaki payroll, including overtime calculated under MOM rules.
