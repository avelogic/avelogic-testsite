How HRKaki stops buddy punching

Buddy punching is when one employee clocks in for another. HRKaki blocks it three ways: a face check at every clock-in, each account locked to the employee's own registered phone, and clock-in times checked against the server, so changing the phone's clock doesn't help.

From clock-in to payslip

Every clock-in, from beacon, GPS or the face terminal, lands in HRKaki payroll in real time, including overtime under MOM rules and attendance incentive policies. With the optional e-Schedule module you can plan shifts and off days, and approved leave updates the roster automatically.

FAQ

Time Attendance System: frequently asked questions

Do staff need to install an app?

For beacon and GPS clock-in, yes. Staff use the HRKaki Attendance app on their own smartphone, and each account is locked to one registered phone. Staff without smartphones can use the 1010-LITE face terminal instead.

How accurate is beacon-based attendance?

The phone must be within 1 metre of the Bluetooth beacon to clock in, so staff have to be physically on site.

Does it work for field and construction teams?

Yes. GPS mode lets staff clock in from any location. Each clock-in records the location and is paired with a selfie face check, so nobody can clock in for someone else.

Does the 1010-LITE face terminal work with face masks?

Yes. The 1010-LITE recognises staff even when they are wearing a mask, verifies in under a second, and also accepts a card for anyone who prefers it.

How many staff can the 1010-LITE terminal hold?

Up to 3,000 faces or cards and 150,000 event logs. It connects by TCP/IP or Wi-Fi and detects faces from 0.3 to 2 metres away.

Does attendance link to payroll and overtime?

Yes. Clock-ins from beacon, GPS and the terminal sync to HRKaki payroll in real time, including overtime calculated under MOM rules.
