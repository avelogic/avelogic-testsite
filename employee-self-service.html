Why self-service saves HR so much time

In most small HR teams, a large share of the week goes to routine requests: "How many days of leave do I have left?", "Can you resend my payslip?", "Where's my IR8A?" Employee self-service moves those questions to the employee, so HR can spend its time on work that needs a person.

What staff can do in HRKaki e-Services

  • eLeave: apply for and cancel leave, check balances, and see the team leave calendar before choosing dates.
  • eClaims: photograph receipts (images are resized automatically), claim against per-staff budgets, and submit in batches. Claims can be reimbursed with payroll.
  • ePayslip and IR8A: view and download payslips and tax forms any time, with past payslips kept online.
  • Profile and assets: view personal details and company assets issued to them.

Singpass login means fewer password resets

Forgotten passwords are one of the most common HR tickets. With Singpass sign-in, staff use the same digital identity they use for government services, so there is no separate password to forget. Company ID and password login stays available for staff who need it, such as some foreign employees.

Approvals that don't get stuck

Each leave or claim request can go through up to two levels of approval. Approvers get an email for every request and can approve or reject in a few taps, and the employee is notified of the outcome.

FAQ

Employee Self-Service: frequently asked questions

Can employees log in to HRKaki e-Services with Singpass?

Yes. Singpass login is built in. Company ID, user ID and password login is also available.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. There are iOS and Android apps, and the web portal adapts to phones, tablets and laptops with nothing to install.

Can claims be paid through payroll?

Yes. Approved claims can be reimbursed together with payroll, or paid separately, and you can set different claim budgets for different staff.

How many approval levels are supported?

Up to two levels, with email notifications to the employee and each approver.
