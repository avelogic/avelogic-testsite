Built for MOE's 2026 rules on phones in school

From January 2026, secondary school students may not use smartphones or smartwatches at any time during school hours, including recess, CCAs and supplementary or enrichment lessons. MOE asks schools to keep devices in designated storage such as lockers, or in school bags. Each school sets its own arrangements, and many have found that collecting, storing and returning phones every day takes real teacher time.

The Avelogic Phone Locker is designed for exactly that routine. Read our guide to phone storage options under MOE's rules for a comparison of lockers, collection bags and other approaches.

Why a keyless locker

  • No keys to lose or lend. Each session has its own 6-digit OTP, held by the teacher in the app.
  • No single keyholder. Any authorised teacher can open it, so a late or absent form teacher doesn't hold up dismissal.
  • Instant accountability. Numbered slots match class register numbers, so an empty slot is obvious.
  • Phones stay safe. Curved dividers keep each phone upright and stop them scratching each other.
  • Fits any classroom. Carry it by the handle between blocks, or mount it on the wall.

FAQ

School Phone Locker: frequently asked questions

How does the phone locker open without a key?

Teachers open it with a 6-digit one-time password (OTP) generated for each session in the Avelogic app, entered on the locker's keypad.

How many phones does one locker hold?

45 phones, in three rows of 15 numbered slots. Each slot is 25 mm wide and 90 mm high.

Can we buy it through GeBIZ?

Yes. Avelogic is an approved Government Supplier on GeBIZ under Supply Head EPU/FUR/10 (Furniture & Racking), Financial Grade S5, valid to December 2027.

Can it be moved between classrooms?

Yes. It has a carry handle and can be used free-standing, or it can be wall-mounted.

Can we see one before buying?

Yes. We offer a free on-site demonstration, scheduled around your school timetable.
