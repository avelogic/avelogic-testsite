Payroll and HR software in Singapore: common questions

Can't find your answer? Call us on +65 6367 1128, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm.

What is HRKaki?

HRKaki is cloud HR and payroll software for Singapore SMEs, built and supported in Singapore by Avelogic Pte Ltd. One system covers employee records, payroll with CPF and IRAS submission, leave, claims, attendance and employee self-service. Every customer also gets a named support person, their kaki.

How does HRKaki keep payroll data secure?

HRKaki protects payroll data in layers. A web application firewall and DDoS protection stop attacks before they reach the system. Employees sign in with Singpass and administrators use two-factor authentication. The database is on a private network with no internet access, and records are encrypted at rest. Servers are continuously scanned for vulnerabilities, and infrastructure changes are audit-logged. Data is stored in Singapore. ImmuniWeb independently graded hrkaki.com A for security and privacy in September 2026, and a detailed security overview is available on request.

Can employees log in to HRKaki with Singpass?

Yes. Employees can sign in to the HRKaki e-Services portal with Singpass, so there is no separate password to forget. Company ID, user ID and password login is still available.

Where is HRKaki data stored?

HRKaki data is stored in Singapore, with daily incremental and weekly full backups.

Does HRKaki calculate CPF and submit to IRAS?

Yes. HRKaki calculates CPF contributions by age band and wage ceiling, plus SDL and CDAC, SINDA, MBMF and ECF. It submits IR8A and Appendix 8A/8B directly through the IRAS API, and prepares IR21 for departing foreign employees. CPF and IRAS rule changes are applied for you.

Does HRKaki file IR8A through the IRAS Auto-Inclusion Scheme?

Yes. IR8A, Appendix 8A/8B and IR21 go to IRAS directly through the Auto-Inclusion Scheme (AIS). AIS is compulsory for employers with 5 or more employees.

Which banks can HRKaki create GIRO salary files for?

DBS, OCBC, UOB, HSBC, Maybank, Citibank, SMBC, MUFG, BNP Paribas and Bank of America.

What does "kaki" mean in HRKaki?

Kaki is Singlish for a buddy, the friend who shows up. In HRKaki it means a named Avelogic person who knows your account and helps with your first payroll, unusual months such as bonuses or backpay, and year-end submissions.

How is HRKaki priced?

HRKaki is priced per employee per month, with no licence fee, no hardware and no long-term lock-in. Data migration is free. Contact Avelogic at sales@avelogic.com or +65 6367 1128 for a quote.

How long does it take to switch to HRKaki?

Most companies run their first payroll on HRKaki within a few weeks. Your kaki migrates employee records, leave balances and salary details for you at no charge, and reviews your first payroll run with you before anything is finalised.

Does HRKaki work on mobile phones?

Yes. The e-Services portal adapts to phone, tablet and desktop, and there are iOS and Android apps. Staff can apply for leave, submit claims, view payslips and IR8A, and clock in from their phones.

What is the Avelogic Phone Locker?

The Avelogic Phone Locker is a keyless steel locker with 45 numbered slots, built for MOE's phone-free school hours. Teachers open it with a 6-digit one-time password (OTP) from an app, so schools have no keys to manage. It is portable or wall-mountable.

Can schools buy the Phone Locker through GeBIZ?

Yes. Avelogic Pte Ltd is an approved Government Supplier on GeBIZ under Supply Head EPU/FUR/10 (Furniture & Racking), Financial Grade S5, valid to December 2027. Schools can buy through their usual GeBIZ channels, and Avelogic offers free on-site demos.
