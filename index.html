Payroll & HR Software

Payroll software for Singapore SMEs, with CPF and IRAS built in

HRKaki is cloud payroll and HR software for Singapore companies. It calculates salaries, CPF, SDL and self-help group contributions, creates GIRO bank files, issues MOM-compliant payslips and submits IR8A to IRAS directly. Leave management and employee records run in the same HR system. When CPF or IRAS rules change, we update it for you, and your kaki checks your first payroll run with you before anything is finalised.

  • Automatic CPF by age band and wage ceiling, plus SDL and CDAC, SINDA, MBMF and ECF
  • MOM-compliant itemised payslips, sent to staff automatically every pay cycle
  • Multiple pay cycles per month, with CPF and statutory contributions recalculated
  • Direct IRAS submission via the IRAS API: IR8A, Appendix 8A/8B, IR21
  • MOM-rule overtime, monthly, daily and ad-hoc allowances, attendance incentives
  • Leave policies with tiered entitlement by grade and length of service
  • HR records: biodata, family, bank details, documents, assets, offences and incidents
  • Reminders for work pass expiry, confirmation, retirement and course expiry

Time Attendance

Time attendance system with mobile clock-in and facial recognition

HRKaki Attendance is a time attendance system for office, site and field staff in Singapore. Staff clock in and out on their phones by Bluetooth beacon at the office or by GPS on site. AWS facial recognition confirms who is scanning, and each account is locked to the staff member's own phone, which stops buddy punching. For fixed sites, add the Model 1010-LITE face recognition terminal. Attendance records flow straight into payroll and overtime.

  • Bluetooth beacon mode: scans only work within about 5 m of the beacon. Needs no power, no internet and no installation.
  • GPS mode: scan in and out from anywhere, and admins see the location and selfie
  • AWS facial recognition plus device locking stops buddy punching
  • Time is checked against both the phone and the server for accurate records
  • Model 1010-LITE terminal: touchless face verification in under 1 second
  • Shift and roster scheduling (optional), linked to leave and payroll

School Phone Locker

Security Upgrade

Payroll data is your most sensitive data, so we protect it in layers.

With the move to HRKaki we rebuilt the platform's security from the network edge to the database. An attacker would have to get past every layer below, and the result has been independently tested.

Staff & admins→Edge protection→Verified sign-in→HRKaki application→Encrypted data
Layer 1 · Edge

Attacks stopped before they arrive

  • Web application firewall blocks common attacks such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting and malicious bots
  • DDoS protection absorbs traffic floods so payroll day stays online
  • All traffic is encrypted in transit with modern TLS
Layer 2 · Identity

Only the right people get in

  • Singpass login for employees, using the national digital identity
  • Two-factor authentication with a one-time password for every administrator
  • Role-based access rights for each HR and finance administrator
Layer 3 · Network

No public route to your data

  • The database sits on a private network and can't be reached from the internet
  • Only the HRKaki application, behind the firewall, can reach it
  • Each client's records are kept separate from every other client's
Layer 4 · Data

Unreadable without the key

  • Employee records are encrypted at rest, with keys held in a managed key service
  • System passwords and keys are kept in a secure vault, never in code
  • Daily incremental and weekly full backups
Layer 5 · Operations

Watched, patched and logged

  • Continuous vulnerability scanning of our servers
  • Security patches applied centrally and promptly
  • Every infrastructure change is recorded in an audit log
  • Least-privilege access for our own team
Layer 6 · Residency & Support

Your data stays in Singapore

  • Hosted on Amazon Web Services in Singapore
  • Managed by Avelogic's Singapore team, Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm
  • A detailed security overview is available on request for procurement and IT teams

Common Questions

HRKaki: Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about HRKaki's features, security and pricing.

What is HRKaki?

HRKaki is cloud HR and payroll software for Singapore SMEs, built and supported in Singapore by Avelogic Pte Ltd. One system covers employee records, payroll with CPF and IRAS submission, leave, claims, attendance and employee self-service. Every customer also gets a named support person, their kaki.

How does HRKaki keep payroll data secure?

HRKaki protects payroll data in layers. A web application firewall and DDoS protection stop attacks before they reach the system. Employees sign in with Singpass and administrators use two-factor authentication. The database is on a private network with no internet access, and records are encrypted at rest. Servers are continuously scanned for vulnerabilities, and infrastructure changes are audit-logged. Data is stored on AWS in Singapore. ImmuniWeb independently graded hrkaki.com A for security and privacy in September 2026, and a detailed security overview is available on request.

Can employees log in to HRKaki with Singpass?

Yes. Employees can sign in to the HRKaki e-Services portal with Singpass, so there is no separate password to forget. Company ID, user ID and password login is still available.

Where is HRKaki data stored?

HRKaki data is stored on Amazon Web Services in the Asia Pacific (Singapore) region, with daily incremental and weekly full backups.

Does HRKaki handle CPF and IRAS submissions?

Yes. HRKaki calculates CPF contributions by age band and wage ceiling, plus SDL and CDAC, SINDA, MBMF and ECF. It submits IR8A, Appendix 8A/8B and IR21 directly through the IRAS API. CPF and IRAS rule changes are applied for you.

What does "kaki" mean in HRKaki?

Kaki is Singlish for a buddy, the friend who shows up. In HRKaki it means a named Avelogic person who knows your account and helps with your first payroll, unusual months such as bonuses or backpay, and year-end submissions.

How is HRKaki priced?

HRKaki is priced per employee per month, with no licence fee, no hardware and no long-term lock-in. Data migration is free. Contact Avelogic at sales@avelogic.com or +65 6367 1128 for a quote.

Can I move from my current payroll system to HRKaki?

Yes. Avelogic migrates employee records, leave balances and salary details for you at no charge, and your kaki reviews your first payroll run with you before anything is finalised.

Does HRKaki work on mobile phones?

Yes. The e-Services portal adapts to phone, tablet and desktop, and there are iOS and Android apps. Staff can apply for leave, submit claims, view payslips and IR8A, and clock in from their phones.

What is the Avelogic Phone Locker?

The Avelogic Phone Locker is a keyless steel locker with 45 numbered slots, built for the MOE phone-free classroom. Teachers open it with a 6-digit one-time password (OTP) from an app, so schools have no keys to manage. It is portable or wall-mountable.

Can schools buy the Phone Locker through GeBIZ?

Yes. Avelogic Pte Ltd is an approved Government Supplier on GeBIZ under Supply Head EPU/FUR/10 (Furniture & Racking), Financial Grade S5, valid to December 2027. Schools can buy through their usual GeBIZ channels, and Avelogic offers free on-site demos.
